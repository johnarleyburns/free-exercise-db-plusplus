# v0.4 batch

Includes both requested phases:

## v0.3.2 cleanup
Exact-name fixes for remaining conventional misses including machine military
press, band hip extension/lift, physioball hip bridge, Cuban press, Air Bike,
Bottoms Up, Otis-Up, flutter kicks, plate/cable rotation movements, push press,
and landmine jammer variants.

## v0.4 complex families
Adds explicit medium-confidence mappings for:
- clean/snatch pulls and full/power/hang/block/split derivatives
- jerks and clean & jerk
- kettlebell clean/snatch/jerk/windmill/sumo-high-pull/thruster families
- muscle-ups and rope climbing
- Atlas stones, keg/sandbag loads, tire flip
- log/circus-bell overhead events, Conan's wheel, power stairs
- battle ropes

Complex mappings use `reviewed_complex_pattern_rule` rather than being promoted
to high confidence.
