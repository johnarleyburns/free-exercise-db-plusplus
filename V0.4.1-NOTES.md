# v0.4.1 unresolved-mapping cleanup

Goal: resolve the final 15 low-confidence records without artificially raising
complex movements to high confidence.

Resolved as medium-confidence named/integrated patterns:
- Alternating Hang Clean
- Dumbbell Clean
- Bottoms-Up Clean From The Hang Position
- Anti-Gravity Press
- Bent Press
- Forward Drag with Press
- Kettlebell Figure 8
- Kettlebell Pass Between The Legs
- Kettlebell Pirate Ships
- Landmine 180's
- One-Arm Kettlebell Para Press
- One-Arm Medicine Ball Slam
- Spider Crawl

Reclassified as non-volume for resistance-set accounting:
- Balance Board
- Wind Sprints

Rationale:
- Balance-board work is balance/proprioceptive training, not a conventional
  resistance set suitable for direct/indirect hypertrophy-set accounting.
- Wind sprints are sprint/cardio/conditioning observations, not resistance sets.
- Bent Press is an integrated leverage/under-the-load movement rather than a
  conventional overhead press.
- Kettlebell Pirate Ships is treated as an integrated shoulder/core movement.
- Remaining ballistic and loaded combinations stay medium confidence via
  `reviewed_complex_pattern_rule`.
