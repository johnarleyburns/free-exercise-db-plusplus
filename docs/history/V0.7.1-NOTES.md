# v0.7.1 — vertical-pull evidence correction

The v0.7 CI failure exposed a valid evidence-granularity inconsistency:

- `Pullups` had an explicit high-confidence override.
- `vertical_pull` was still marked `indirect_support`.
- The cited source (`pullup_emg_2017`, PMID 28828073) is actually a direct
  pull-up EMG study.

v0.7.1 therefore changes:

- `vertical_pull` -> `supported`
- `horizontal_pull` remains `indirect_support`

This preserves the evidence-driven confidence policy rather than weakening the
CI check or demoting the reviewed Pullups override.

Converter version: 0.7.1
Schema version remains 0.3.0.
