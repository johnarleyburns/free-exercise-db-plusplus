# Free Exercise DB++ v1.5.1

This patch release corrects one semantic inconsistency in relationship
coverage comparison.

- `RelationshipRegistry.compare_exercise_coverage()` now reads effective-set
  credits through the canonical analysis policy and requires authoritative
  `metadata.setCredits`.
- The silent `1.0 / 0.5 / 0.0` fallback was removed.
- No exercise-family memberships, family identifiers, relationship schema, or
  exact interoperability mappings changed.

The relationship artifact schema remains independently versioned at `0.1.0`.
Family membership remains descriptive taxonomy, not physiological equivalence
or automatic substitution advice.
