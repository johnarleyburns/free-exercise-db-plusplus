# Free Exercise DB++ v1.7.1

Free Exercise DB++ v1.7.1 is a narrowly scoped semantic-hardening patch for
TrainingState and advisory progression.

It corrects direction-specific effort reason codes: lower RPE is too little
effort and higher RPE is too much; lower RIR is too much effort and higher RIR
is too little. The underlying hold and double-progression success rules are
unchanged.

TrainingState now exposes canonical exercise prescription adherence summaries,
quantitative per-prescription skipped and substitution counts, and an explicit
distinction between latest performance and the deterministic recent performance
series. `recentReps`, `recentLoads`, `recentRPE`, and `recentRIR` remain
backward-compatible latest-occurrence fields.

Progression policy maps are evaluated directly in PLAN session/exercise order.
`hold-v1` and `double-progression-v1` remain advisory, explainable, and
non-mutating. PLAN, ACTUAL, TARGET, and independent schema versions are
unchanged.
